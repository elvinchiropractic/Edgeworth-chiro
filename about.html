Meet your chiropractors

Two chiropractors, one clinic in Edgeworth.

Edgeworth Chiropractic Care brings together Dr Shane Moss, long-time chiropractor at Lorn Chiropractic in Maitland, and Dr Joshua Elvin. Together they lead this Arnott Street clinic with the same hands-on, evidence-based approach their existing patients already know.

The team

Two clinics' worth of experience, now together

Dr Shane Moss

Lead Chiropractor, Edgeworth Chiropractic Care

Dr Moss has built his career at Lorn Chiropractic in Maitland around manual adjustment and soft tissue work, with a particular focus on custom orthotics, family care and treating workplace injuries among Hunter Valley mining and trade workers. At Edgeworth, he brings that same hands-on technique and straightforward approach to a new community.

Dr Joshua Elvin

Chiropractor, Edgeworth Chiropractic Care

Dr Elvin's practice is built around evidence-based, customised treatment plans rather than a one-size-fits-all protocol. He sees patients at Edgeworth, treating everything from back and neck pain to sports injuries, sciatica and posture-related pain.

Our approach

Evidence-based care, explained in plain English

No jargon, no scare tactics. Just a clear explanation of what's going on and what your options are, so you can make an informed decision about your own care.

Assess

A thorough first visit covering history, posture and movement, so treatment addresses the cause, not just the symptom.

Treat

Hands-on manual adjustment and soft tissue work, tailored to your body and your tolerance for treatment.

Maintain

Practical advice and, where useful, exercises you can do at home, so you need fewer visits over time, not more.
