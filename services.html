What we treat

Six ways we help you move without pain.

Every treatment plan starts with a proper assessment. From there, we focus on whichever of these areas is actually behind your pain, not a generic package.

Manual Adjustment

Our core technique: hands-on manual adjustment and soft tissue work to correct misalignment in your spine and joints. It's precise, low-force where needed, and adapted to your comfort and history.

Musculoskeletal Care

Treatment for the everyday pain that builds up over time: headaches, migraines, sciatica, pins and needles, shoulder problems, plantar fasciitis and tennis elbow.

Custom Orthotics

Custom-molded orthotics fitted in-clinic at a competitive price, with health fund rebates considered wherever possible, so better foot and posture support doesn't blow the budget.

Family Care

Gentle, age-appropriate chiropractic care across every stage of life, from infants and growing kids through to parents and elderly patients.

Work Injuries

Assessment and treatment for workplace-related pain and injury, with particular experience supporting mining and trade workers from across the Lake Macquarie and Maitland area.

Sports Injury

Assessment and hands-on treatment for sports-related injuries, focused on getting weekend warriors and competitive athletes back to their sport as quickly and safely as possible.

Not sure which service you need?

That's what the first visit is for. Give us a call and we'll point you in the right direction.
